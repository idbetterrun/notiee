Notiee Privacy Policy

Version: v1.0.5   Effective Date: June 24, 2026

1. Introduction

We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy aims to transparently explain what information Notiee (hereinafter "the App") collects, how it uses that information, and how it protects your data security.

Core Principle: Your data stays in your hands. This App is a local-first utility application. All your records, notes, to-dos, and conversations are stored on your own device.

4. Data Storage and Security

MeasureDescription
Local-FirstAll records and to-dos are stored locally on device by default, not dependent on the cloud
Keychain EncryptionAPI Keys are stored using iOS Keychain (hardware-level Secure Enclave encryption), not written to regular files, and automatically cleared upon app uninstallation
HTTPS TransportAll AI service calls use HTTPS encrypted transport
No Cloud RetentionNotiee does not operate its own servers and does not collect or store user content data
iCloud SecurityIf the user manually enables iCloud sync, data is stored in the user's personal iCloud Drive, protected by Apple's security framework
WebFetch SecurityUses ephemeral network sessions (non-persistent cookies/cache), built-in SSRF protection, blocks access to local and intranet addresses (localhost, 10.x, 172.16-31.x, 192.168.x, etc.)
On-Device Semantic ComputingUses Apple NLEmbedding on-device vector engine by default; vector computation for photo note search and "Deep Association" related recommendations is performed entirely on-device and not uploaded to any server
No Unsolicited Network RequestsExcept for user-initiated AI processing, Spark conversations, WebFetch, or iCloud sync, this App does not spontaneously send data to any server

5.1 Does not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties.

5.2 User data may only be disclosed under the following circumstances: required by laws and regulations; to protect the legal rights of the developer or others; with the user's explicit consent.

5.3 Data for AI service calls is sent to AI service providers chosen by the user and is subject to those providers' own privacy policies, unrelated to this App.

5.4 Web content fetched by WebFetch is provided by the target website and is subject to that website's terms of use.

7. Protection of Minors

7.1 This App is designed for students and professionals and is not specifically targeted at children under 14.

7.2 If a guardian discovers that a minor is using this App without consent, they may contact us using the contact information at the end of this policy to delete the data.

8. Spark AI Assistant Privacy

Spark is the built-in AI assistant of this App with the following privacy features:

8.1 Conversation Content: Spark's conversation messages (user input and AI responses) are sent to the user-configured AI service provider for processing. Conversation history is stored locally on the device.

8.2 Agent Mode: When using Agent mode, Spark may read the user's photo notes, schedules, to-dos, and other local data as operational context, and related data may be sent to the AI service provider with the request.

8.3 WebFetch: In Agent mode, web content may be fetched based on user instructions, using ephemeral secure sessions with no persistent network data.

8.4 Long-Term Memory: Spark's memory system (user preferences, habits, etc.) is stored locally on the device and not uploaded to any server.

8.5 Privacy Consent: When using Spark for the first time, users must read and agree to the Spark Privacy Consent.
