6. AI Features Special Terms

6.1 How AI Services Are Provided: Users configure their own API Key to connect to the AI service provider of their choice (such as OpenAI, DeepSeek, Anthropic, Alibaba Cloud Bailian, Volcano Engine, MiniMax, etc.). Notiee does not provide its own AI cloud services.

6.2 Data Processing:

  • Text and images are sent to the user-configured AI service provider for processing. Notiee does not intercept or store them in transit.
  • Spark AI Assistant conversation content (including user messages and AI replies) is sent to the user-configured AI service provider.
  • In Agent mode, Spark may call tools to access the user's local data such as Snapnotes, schedules, and To-dos. Relevant data may be sent as context to the AI service provider.
  • The semantic search feature computes vectors locally on the device by default. Snapnote text is sent to the AI service provider's Embedding API only when the user manually enables "High-Quality Cloud Search" in Labs. The "Deep Recall" related recommendations on the detail page always use local vectors and are never transmitted externally.

6.3 WebFetch Feature: In Agent mode, Spark may fetch web page content from a specified URL based on user instructions. This feature uses temporary network sessions (no persistent cookies/cache) and includes built-in security protections that block access to local and intranet addresses.

6.4 User Responsibility: Users must obtain their own API Key and comply with the applicable terms of service and policies of the corresponding AI service provider.

6.5 AI Output Disclaimer: AI-generated summaries, To-dos, conversation replies, and other content are for reference only and do not constitute professional advice (including but not limited to legal, medical, financial, or other professional advice). Users should independently assess the accuracy and applicability of AI outputs.
