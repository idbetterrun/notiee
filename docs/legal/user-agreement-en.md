## 6. AI Features Special Terms

6.1 **AI Service Provision**: Users configure their own API keys to connect to AI service providers of their choice (such as OpenAI, DeepSeek, Anthropic, Alibaba Cloud Bailian, Volcano Engine, MiniMax, etc.). Notiee does not provide its own AI cloud services.

6.2 **Data Processing Description**:
   - Text and images will be sent to the AI service provider configured by the user for processing. Notiee does not intercept or store them in transit.
   - Spark AI Assistant conversation content (including user messages and AI responses) will be sent to the AI service provider configured by the user.
   - In Agent mode, Spark may invoke tools to access the user's local data such as photo notes, schedules, and to-dos. Relevant data may be sent to the AI service provider as context.
   - Semantic search functionality computes vectors locally on the device by default. Photo note text is only sent to the Embedding API of the user-configured AI service provider when the user manually enables "High-Quality Cloud Search" in Labs. The "Deep Association" related recommendations on detail pages always use local vectors and are not transmitted externally.

6.3 **WebFetch Feature**: In Agent mode, Spark can fetch web content from a specified URL based on user instructions. This feature uses temporary network sessions (cookies/cache are not persisted) and includes built-in security mechanisms that prohibit access to local and intranet addresses.

6.4 **User Responsibility**: Users are responsible for obtaining their own API keys and complying with the terms of service and service agreements of the corresponding AI service providers.

6.5 **AI Output Disclaimer**: AI-generated summaries, to-dos, conversation responses, and other content are for reference only and do not constitute professional advice (including but not limited to professional advice in legal, medical, financial, and other fields). Users should independently assess the accuracy and applicability of AI outputs.
